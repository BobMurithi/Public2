Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Can different exhibitor packages be registered?

Yes - exhibitors select their package in the flow, pricing is confirmed and the booking is locked automatically.

Does it support online payments?

Yes - via M-Pesa or Stripe. Payment confirmation triggers the ticket or exhibitor brief automatically.

Will visitors get reminders?

Yes - two reminders before the event with date, time and venue details.

Can we use it for conference events too?

Absolutely - the same flow works for conferences, workshops and any event with exhibitor and visitor registration.
