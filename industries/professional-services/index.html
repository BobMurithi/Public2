Frequently asked questions

Everything Professional Services businesses ask before getting started.

How long does it take to set up Professional Services automation?

Most automations go live within 3-7 days. We handle the entire setup, integration and testing before we hand it over to you.

Do I need a technical team to use it?

No. Bobsave builds, hosts and maintains every workflow for you. You receive simple reports and dashboards - no technical skills required.

Can it work with the tools I already use?

Yes. We connect to WhatsApp, email, Google Sheets, calendars and most popular business tools, and we customise integrations to your existing stack.

How much does Professional Services automation cost?

Individual automations start at KSh 3,500 setup plus KSh 1,500 per month, with packages offering better value for complete solutions.

What happens after launch?

We provide training, monitor the workflow, and maintain it ongoing - including updates and support whenever you need help.
