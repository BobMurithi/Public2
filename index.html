Frequently Asked Questions

What is business automation?+
Business automation uses software to perform repetitive tasks automatically instead of requiring someone to do them manually every time.
Do I need technical knowledge?+
No. We handle the technical setup, integrations, hosting, testing and maintenance for you.
What can you automate?+
Almost any repetitive digital process involving forms, email, WhatsApp, CRMs, spreadsheets, databases, payments, APIs, AI, notifications or business applications.
Do I need to replace my existing software?+
Usually, no. We aim to connect the tools you're already using rather than forcing you to replace them.
Do you use n8n?+
Yes. We use n8n and other technologies to build flexible, reliable automation workflows suited to your business requirements.
Can you build custom automation?+
Yes. If your process doesn't fit one of our standard solutions, we can design a custom workflow around your requirements.
Do you host the automation?+
Yes. Our plans can include managed hosting, monitoring and maintenance so you don't have to manage the infrastructure yourself.
What happens if my automation stops working?+
Depending on your plan, we monitor your workflows, investigate failures, and provide maintenance and support to get them running again.
Can I start with one automation?+
Yes. Start with one process, see the results, and expand your automation as your business grows.
How do I get started?+
Contact us and tell us what repetitive task or business process you'd like to automate. We'll help you determine the best solution.
