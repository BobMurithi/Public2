Problem

Property enquiries are hot for 24 hours - agents who reply late lose the buyer. Messages from different listings pile up, and follow-ups are forgotten.

Solution

Instant, relevant property responses per enquiry, automatic qualification of budget and area, viewing booking, and timed follow-ups that keep every buyer engaged.

Benefits

  • Reply to enquiries in under a minute
  • Buyers matched to the right properties

Frequently asked questions

Can every listing get its own flow?

Yes - each project or listing can send its tailored details while sharing the same qualification and follow-up engine.

How is a viewing booked?

The flow finds a slot with your agents and confirms it in both the buyer chat and your calendar.

What about buyers who go quiet?

They enter a gentle follow-up sequence so you stay top of mind when they are ready to move.
