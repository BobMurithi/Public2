Frequently asked questions

Does it replace our sales people?

No - it helps them. The automation handles first contact, qualification and reminders, so your team focuses on closing ready buyers.

Where do leads come from?

Wherever they appear: WhatsApp, website forms, Facebook/Instagram ads or a Google Sheet you already use.

What happens to old leads?

Cold leads get a timed follow-up sequence, and everything is logged so you can see exactly what each contact received.
