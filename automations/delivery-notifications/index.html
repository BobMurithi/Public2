Problem

Customers constantly ask 'where is my order?'. Manual status updates eat driver and dispatch time, and vague updates damage trust in your delivery promise.

Solution

Automatic status messages at every milestone, tracking links, and a confirmation loop that closes the loop - so customers always know exactly where their order is.

Benefits

  • Fewer 'where is my order?' messages
  • Customers trust your delivery promise
  • Drivers stop reporting statuses manually
  • Full delivery trail recorded

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with our courier partner?

Yes - we connect to your dispatch sheet or logistics system and send updates exactly when milestones are reached.

Can customers reply or give feedback?

They can. Delivery confirmations and feedback messages flow back into your records.

What if a delivery fails?

The automation raises a re-delivery request and notifies your team so nothing is left hanging.
