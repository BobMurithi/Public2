Problem

Registration teams drown in WhatsApp messages and spreadsheets. Double entries, lost details and late confirmations frustrate exhibitors and slow down event day.

Solution

A registration workflow that captures, confirms, tickets and reminds every participant automatically, with one clean attendee list updated in real time.

Benefits

  • Exhibitors registered without back-and-forth
  • Tickets and confirmations delivered instantly
  • Real-time attendee list, zero duplicates
  • Team focuses on the event, not admin

How it works

Frequently asked questions

Can exhibitors register different booth sizes?

Yes - your packages can be selected in the flow, and pricing is confirmed automatically before the booking is locked in.

What about payments?

We verify payment confirmations from M-Pesa or your gateway and release the ticket automatically.

Do attendees get reminders before the event?

They do, including date, venue and what to bring - all automatic.
