Problem

No-shows burn time and money. Manual reminder calls are tedious, get forgotten, and leave already-open slots empty.

Solution

Automatic WhatsApp and SMS reminders at the right moments, one-tap confirm or reschedule, and automatic slot re-opening when a client cancels.

Benefits

  • Fewer empty appointments
  • Staff stop making reminder calls
  • Clients confirm or reschedule in one tap
  • Clear picture of daily attendance

Frequently asked questions

Do reminders work with SMS too?

Yes. We can send on WhatsApp, SMS or both, so clients who are not on WhatsApp are still covered.

Where do clients confirm?

They simply reply in the WhatsApp chat - a confirmation is logged and your schedule updates automatically.

Can reminders be branded for our business?

Fully. Messages go out in your business name with your tone and wording.
