Appointment Booking Automation

Let clients book appointments from WhatsApp or your website, 24/7.

Problem

Staff spend hours answering the same booking messages, double-bookings happen, and clients who try to reach you after hours are lost to your competitors.

Solution

A booking workflow that receives requests from WhatsApp and your website, confirms availability automatically, sends instant confirmation, and syncs every appointment into your calendar, CRM or spreadsheet.

Benefits

  • Bookings captured 24/7 without extra staff
  • Instant confirmation builds client trust
  • Double-booking and missed calls eliminated
  • Every booking logged centrally for reporting

Frequently asked questions

Can clients really book from WhatsApp?

Yes. Clients send a message, the workflow checks availability and confirms their booking - then adds it to your calendar automatically.

What if we already use a booking app?

We connect to your existing tool (Calendly, SimplePractice or a spreadsheet) so nothing gets replaced unless you want it to.

Can it handle after-hours bookings?

That is exactly where it shines. Every message gets answered instantly, even at midnight, so no enquiry is ever lost.
