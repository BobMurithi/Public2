Frequently asked questions

Which payment methods are supported?

M-Pesa via the Daraja API, bank transfers and any gateway you already use. Confirmations are picked up automatically.

How are parents reminded?

Polite WhatsApp messages before and after the due date, with optional SMS fallback.

Can it show who has not paid?

Yes - the ledger view always reflects outstanding balances per student, per class or per term.
